Ads & privacy

Applies to mutaafaziz.com and the apps and games under it (OUTATIME 88, OpenAero DFW, COLD GAS, KIN, Chalk Talk, Hearth, Library) and to 18-0.co. Updated 30 October 2026.

Why there are ads

These apps are free and made by one person. Ads pay for the servers and the time. Every ad is labelled Ad or Sponsored, and tapping that label brings you here.

Two kinds of ads

  • Google ads (AdSense and H5 Games Ads), loaded from Google's servers. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn personalised ads off at Google Ads Settings, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first.
  • Sponsor ads (images or short videos) served by this site itself. These use no cookies and no tracking. We only count totals, such as "campaign X was seen 120 times on Tuesday," with no visitor ID, IP address or device information.

Usage analytics

The apps and games for everyone (not the kids' games, Moon Club or the Metaphases kids pages) count how they are used with PostHog, sent through mutaafaziz.com: pages viewed, clicks, scroll depth, approximate location (city level) and session replays with everything you type hidden. Your IP address is shortened before it leaves mutaafaziz.com, no cookies are set (a random anonymous ID is kept in your browser's storage), and nothing is tied to your name or email. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, no analytics load at all.

Kids' games

OUTATIME 88, OpenAero DFW and KIN are played by kids. In those games:

  • Google ad requests are tagged child-directed, so Google serves only non-personalised ads and doesn't build profiles.
  • Google ads appear only outside gameplay, on menus and between rounds. Ads inside the game are reviewed sponsor ads.
  • Sponsor ads in these games can't be clicked through to another site, and they never ask for sign-ups or personal information.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional, and the game works fully without them.
  • No usage analytics run in these games.

What we never do

We don't sell personal data, run third-party trackers beyond the Google ad code and the usage analytics described above, or ask for names, emails or birthdays in order to show ads.
